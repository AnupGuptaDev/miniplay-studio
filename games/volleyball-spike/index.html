How to Play Volleyball Spike

  1. Press Start to begin a rally.
  2. Watch the ball approach the net.
  3. Press SPIKE when the ball reaches the hitting zone.
  4. Good timing scores a point; mistimed hits are returned.
  5. Play ten rallies and try to build the highest score.

Tip: Wait until the ball is near the top of its arc before spiking.
