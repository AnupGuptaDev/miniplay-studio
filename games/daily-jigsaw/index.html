How to Play Picture Jigsaw Puzzle

  1. Choose Easy (9 pieces), Medium (16 pieces) or Hard (25 pieces).
  2. Press New puzzle to see the picture cut into jigsaw pieces.
  3. Drag a loose piece from the tray onto the matching position on the board.
  4. If it is close to the correct place, it snaps into position. Incorrect pieces return to the tray.
  5. Complete the whole picture to finish. Your completion time and best time are saved on your device.

Tip: Start with the corners and edges, then use colours and details in the picture to match the middle pieces.
