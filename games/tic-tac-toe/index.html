How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe

  1. Choose Play computer to play against the computer, or Two players to play with a friend.
  2. Players take turns selecting an empty square. X goes first.
  3. Be the first to get three of your marks in a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line.
  4. If all nine squares are filled and nobody has three in a row, the round is a draw.
  5. Select New round to play again. The score counters track wins and draws during your visit.

Tip: Try to make a line while blocking your opponent from making one.
