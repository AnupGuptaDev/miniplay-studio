How to Play Golf Putt Challenge

  1. Press Start to begin the putting challenge.
  2. Choose a power level using the slider.
  3. Press PUTT to roll toward the hole.
  4. A balanced power earns a successful putt.
  5. Complete five holes and try to finish under par.

Tip: Shorter putts need less power; avoid using maximum power every time.
