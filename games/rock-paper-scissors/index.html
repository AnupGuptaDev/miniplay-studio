How to Play Rock Paper Scissors

  1. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
  2. The computer makes its choice at the same time.
  3. Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, and Paper beats Rock.
  4. If both choices are the same, the round is a draw.
  5. Keep playing to build your score. Select Reset score to clear the counters.

Tip: There is no guaranteed strategy against a random choice, so enjoy the challenge!
