How to Play Missing Item Mystery

  1. Press Start and study the objects shown on screen.
  2. Memorise every item before the timer ends.
  3. One object disappears. Choose the missing item from the options.
  4. Correct answers earn points; each round gets a little harder.
  5. Complete ten rounds and beat your best score.

Tip: Say the objects quietly in your head from left to right to remember them.
