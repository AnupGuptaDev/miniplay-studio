How to Play Tennis Rally

  1. Press Start to serve the ball.
  2. Watch for the incoming ball and tap Return at the right moment.
  3. A well-timed return keeps the rally going and adds a point.
  4. Missing the ball ends the rally.
  5. Try to beat your longest rally.

Tip: Pay attention to the ball cue and react quickly.
