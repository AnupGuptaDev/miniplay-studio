How to Play Football Penalty Shootout

  1. Press Start shootout. You get five penalty attempts.
  2. Click or tap inside the goal to choose where you want to aim. A target marker shows your shot direction.
  3. Press SHOOT! Your player kicks the ball toward the selected spot.
  4. The computer goalkeeper chooses a dive direction and tries to save your shot.
  5. Score as many goals as possible from five penalties. Your best score is saved.

Tip: Aim for the corners, but the goalkeeper can sometimes guess your direction!
