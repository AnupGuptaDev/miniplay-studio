How to Play Football Penalty Shootout

  1. Start a five-shot penalty shootout.
  2. Choose Left, Center or Right to aim.
  3. The goalkeeper will dive in one direction.
  4. Score when your shot goes to a different direction from the goalkeeper.
  5. Try to score as many goals as possible from five shots.

Tip: Mix up your shot directions to keep the goalkeeper guessing.
