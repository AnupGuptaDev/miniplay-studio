How to Play Snake Rush

  1. Guide the snake around the board to collect the food.
  2. Each food item adds 10 points and makes your snake longer.
  3. Avoid hitting the walls or running into your own snake.
  4. Keep collecting food and try to beat your best score. Select Restart to begin again.

Controls: Use the arrow keys or W, A, S, D on a keyboard. On a phone or tablet, swipe the board or use the direction buttons.
