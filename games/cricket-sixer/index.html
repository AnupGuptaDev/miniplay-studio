How to Play Cricket Sixer

  1. Press Start innings. The computer bowler automatically bowls six deliveries.
  2. Watch the ball travel from the bowler toward your batter.
  3. Press HIT THE BALL when it reaches the batting zone near your player.
  4. Excellent timing can score a SIX, good timing a FOUR, or smaller runs. Mistimed shots can miss or lose a wicket.
  5. Score as many runs as possible in six balls. Beat your saved best score.

Tip: Don't swing too early. Let the ball get close to the batter before hitting.
