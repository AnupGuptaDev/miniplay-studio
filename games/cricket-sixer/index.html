How to Play Cricket Sixer

  1. Press Start to begin an over of six balls.
  2. Watch the ball move toward the batter.
  3. Tap HIT when the ball reaches the green timing zone.
  4. Better timing earns more runs: 0, 1, 2, 4 or 6.
  5. Score as many runs as possible from six deliveries.

Tip: Wait until the ball is close to the batter for the best chance of a boundary.
