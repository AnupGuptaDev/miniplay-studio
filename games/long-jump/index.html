How to Play Long Jump Challenge

  1. Press Run-up to build speed. The power meter increases as you run.
  2. Press Jump near the take-off point for the best result.
  3. Jumping too early or too late reduces your distance.
  4. Complete three attempts and try to beat your personal best.

Tip: Wait for the run-up meter to reach the green zone before jumping.
