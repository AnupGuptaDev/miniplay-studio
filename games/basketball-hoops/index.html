How to Play Basketball Hoops

  1. Start a session of ten shots.
  2. Adjust the strength slider before each shot.
  3. Press Shoot to release the ball.
  4. A well-balanced shot scores a basket; other attempts miss.
  5. Try to make the highest score out of ten shots.

Tip: Try a middle strength first, then adjust based on the result.
