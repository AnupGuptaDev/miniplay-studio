How to Play Hurdles Race

  1. Press Start to begin the 100-metre hurdles.
  2. Tap JUMP when each hurdle gets close.
  3. A well-timed jump clears the hurdle and adds points.
  4. Colliding with a hurdle costs time.
  5. Clear all hurdles to finish the race.

Tip: Jump when the hurdle marker enters the highlighted zone.
