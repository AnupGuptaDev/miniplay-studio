How to Play Running Race

  1. Press Start to line up against the computer runner.
  2. Tap the TAP TO RUN button repeatedly, or press the Space bar, to make your runner move faster.
  3. The computer races automatically alongside you.
  4. Reach the finish line before the computer to win. If the computer finishes first, you lose.
  5. Press Start / Restart to race again and try to improve your result.

Tip: Keep a quick, steady tapping rhythm. Every tap gives your runner a burst of speed.
