How to Play Running Race

  1. Press Start to begin running along the track.
  2. Jump over hurdles and duck under overhead barriers.
  3. Each second you survive adds to your distance.
  4. Hit an obstacle and the race ends. Restart to try again.

Controls: Press Space or ↑ to jump, ↓ to duck, or use the on-screen buttons on touch devices.
