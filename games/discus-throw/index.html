How to Play Discus Throw

  1. Press Start for three discus attempts.
  2. Set the release power and angle.
  3. Press THROW to launch the discus.
  4. Each throw displays its distance.
  5. Beat your personal best across three attempts.

Tip: Try a balanced release angle with high power.
