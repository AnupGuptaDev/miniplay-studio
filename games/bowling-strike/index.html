How to Play Bowling Strike

  1. Press Start for a fresh frame.
  2. Choose your lane aim with the slider.
  3. Press ROLL to send the ball down the lane.
  4. Your aim influences how many pins fall.
  5. Play ten frames and chase a perfect score.

Tip: Keep your aim near the centre for a consistent shot.
