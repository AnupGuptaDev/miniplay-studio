How to Play Bubble Burst

  1. Press Start to release rows of colourful bubbles from the top.
  2. A bubble is loaded into the launcher at the bottom.
  3. Move your mouse or finger to aim, then click or tap the playfield to shoot.
  4. Connect three or more bubbles of the same colour to burst them.
  5. Unattached bubbles drop away. Clear the board before the bubbles reach the launcher.

Tip: Aim at the walls to bounce shots into tricky spaces and create bigger drops.
