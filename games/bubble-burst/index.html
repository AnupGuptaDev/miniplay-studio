Tap a group of two or more matching bubbles to pop them.

How to Play Bubble Burst

  1. Tap any bubble to select its connected group of the same colour.
  2. Groups of two or more connected bubbles can be popped.
  3. Bubbles above fall down to fill empty spaces, and columns close up.
  4. Earn more points by popping larger groups. Clear as many bubbles as you can.

Tip: Plan your pops to create bigger colour groups.
