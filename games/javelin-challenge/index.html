How to Play Javelin Challenge

  1. Set your power and launch angle using the sliders.
  2. Press Throw to launch the javelin in this simple arcade simulation.
  3. Your result is calculated as a game score in metres.
  4. Adjust your settings and try to beat your best distance.

Tip: Experiment with different combinations — the best game result may surprise you!
