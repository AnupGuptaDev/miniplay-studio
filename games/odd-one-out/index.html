How to Play Odd One Out

  1. Press Start to see a group of five symbols.
  2. Four symbols share a category or feature; one is different.
  3. Select the odd one out.
  4. Each correct answer earns 10 points. There are ten rounds.
  5. Read the feedback to learn why the item was different.

Tip: Look for shared categories, shapes, colours or meanings — not just the pictures.
